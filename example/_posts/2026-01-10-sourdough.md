title: Baking sourdough bread at home
tags: [baking]
---
A sourdough loaf rises thanks to wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria living in the starter. Feed the starter with equal weights of flour and water a few hours before mixing the dough.

Give the dough a long, cool bulk fermentation, fold it every half hour, then shape it and proof it overnight in the fridge. Bake in a preheated dutch oven for a crackly crust.
